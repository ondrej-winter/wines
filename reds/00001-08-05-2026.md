## Final Summary

This is a **2024 DeAngelis Campo di Marte Montepulciano d’Abruzzo Biologico**, a dry Italian red from Abruzzo made from **Montepulciano**. From my perspective, the wine appears **youthful and deep ruby**, with a fruit profile centered mainly on **plum**, plus **earth** and a slight **rustic edge**. Structurally, it feels **dry**, **medium to medium-plus in body**, with **noticeable tannin** and a **medium-plus to long finish**.

For me, the main issue is the **finish**: it comes across as **slightly bitter, drying, and a bit too persistent**. That makes it feel **less smooth and more grippy** than I prefer. Overall: **sound wine, but not an ideal personal fit**.
