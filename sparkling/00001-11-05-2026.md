## Final Summary

This Contarini Valse Bianco Vino Spumante Extra Dry comes across as a simple Italian sparkling white with a slightly sweet impression, which is plausible for the “Extra Dry” category. From my perspective, the main problem is not just the sweetness, but the weak effervescence: there is very little sparkle, and the bubbles disappear very quickly. Overall, it feels soft, low-energy, and less refreshing than I would want from a sparkling wine.
